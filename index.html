Sunday cook

  1. Oven 400°F. Start brown rice (4 cups dry) and barley.
  2. Sheet-pan egg + sweet potato bake.
  3. One pot chili. Other pot stew.
  4. Oven: turkey meatballs + green beans. Sauce on the stove.
  5. Oven: lemon chicken + veg. Pack with barley.
  6. Skillet: fajita chicken + peppers over rice.
  7. Cool in shallow containers within 2 hours.
  8. Salmon Monday night unless you want it Sunday — then eat by Tuesday.
