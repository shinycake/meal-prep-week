Tonight’s session

Cook these 3 in parallel

Egg bake, lemon chicken barley, and fajitas — the three dishes you actually bought. One ~75 minute pass. Tap a row to check it off. Checks stay on this phone.

~75 minutes 16 containers Chicken first, then eggs

This is not the full seven-dish Sunday. Chili, stew, salmon, and meatballs stay off the stove — those lists were never checked off. Fajitas use extra barley instead of brown rice. Barley cooks in water (broth was on the chili list). Skip cilantro and feta. Garlic only if you already have a head.

Stations

Oven425°F for lemon chicken, then down to 400°F for the egg bake. Never both temps at once.
Pot2 cups rinsed pearl barley + 6 cups water. Simmer 30–35 min until chewy. That’s 4 barley bowls plus ~2 cups cooked for fajitas.
SkilletFajita chicken, then peppers and onion. One pan.
Sheet pansPan A: lemon chicken + veg at 425°. Pan B: egg bake at 400° after chicken is packed.

Gear

  • 1 pot with lid (barley)
  • 1 large skillet (fajitas)
  • 2 sheet pans (lemon chicken, then egg bake)
  • Food thermometer — chicken to 165°F
  • ~16 shallow meal containers
  • Cutting board, knife, 2 mixing bowls, tongs, spatula

Keep the two chickens apart

  • 1¼ lb boneless skinless breast → lemon sheet pan (pound even)
  • 1½ lb boneless skinless breast, strips → fajita skillet

Pull from the fridge (bought)

  • Chicken breast — 1¼ lb + 1½ lb, kept separate
  • Eggs — 1 dozen (need 10)
  • 1% milk or unsweetened almond milk — ¼ cup
  • Sweet potatoes — 2 medium (~1½ lb)
  • Baby spinach — 1 small box (2 cups chopped)
  • Red bell pepper — 1 (egg bake)
  • Bell peppers — 3 mixed colors (fajitas)
  • Onion — 1 small (egg bake) + 1 large (fajitas)
  • Red onion — 1 small (barley bowls)
  • Broccoli — 1 medium head
  • Carrots — ½ lb
  • Zucchini — 1
  • Lemon — 1 (zest and juice)
  • Limes — 2
  • Pearl barley — 2 cups dry from the bag
  • Black beans, no-salt-added — 1 can, rinsed
  • Avocado — 1, sliced only when you eat
  • Parsley or chives if you bought them (optional)

Pantry

  • Olive oil — 6 tablespoons total (2 per dish)
  • Smoked paprika, sweet paprika, cumin, oregano, black pepper, pinch of salt
  • Garlic — 8 cloves if you have a head (2 egg + 3 barley + 3 fajita). Skip if not.

Do not use tonight

  • Brown rice — never bought (on the salmon list). Extra barley stands in.
  • Chicken broth — never bought (on the chili list). Barley cooks in water.
  • Cilantro — never bought (on the chili list). Skip.
  • Feta — still open on Todoist. Skip. Zero cheese.
  • Turkey, stew beef, salmon, green beans, crushed tomatoes, chili beans — not bought. Do not start those recipes.

0:00 — fire the stove

  • OvenHeat to 425°F.
  • PotRinse 2 cups pearl barley. Add 6 cups water. Simmer lid ajar 30–35 minutes until chewy, then drain.1 cup of that dry barley is the 4 lemon bowls. The extra cup is ~2+ cups cooked for fajitas instead of rice.

0:00–0:10 — cut everything

Do this while the oven heats and barley starts. Don’t whisk the eggs yet.

  • Lemon veg1 medium head broccoli → florets. ½ lb carrots → sticks. 1 zucchini → thick half-moons. 1 small red onion → wedges. Toss with 1 Tbsp oil, half the garlic (if any), 1 tsp dried oregano, black pepper.
  • Lemon chickenPound 1¼ lb breast even. Toss with 1 Tbsp oil, remaining garlic, zest of 1 lemon, ½ tsp sweet paprika, ½ tsp black pepper, pinch of salt. Save the lemon juice for packing.
  • Fajita chickenSlice 1½ lb into strips. Toss with 1 Tbsp oil, garlic if you have it, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp sweet paprika, 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp black pepper.
  • Fajita veg3 mixed peppers + 1 large onion, sliced. Rinse the black beans. Have 2 limes ready.
  • Egg bake laterCube 2 medium sweet potatoes ½-inch. Dice 1 red pepper + 1 small onion. Chop 2 cups spinach. Park them. Eggs stay in the carton until 0:40.

0:10 — lemon chicken in the oven

  • Sheet pan A: veg one side, lemon chicken the other. Roast 22–28 minutes until chicken is 165°F.

0:10–0:35 — fajitas in the skillet (same window)

  • Hot skillet. Cook fajita chicken in two batches to 165°F. Set aside. Don’t crowd the pan.
  • Same pan: remaining 1 Tbsp oil, peppers + onion 6–8 minutes until soft with browned edges.
  • Barley should finish around 0:30–0:35. Drain. Cool slightly.

0:35 — pack both chicken dishes

  • FajitasChicken back in the skillet. Add rinsed beans + juice of 2 limes. Skip cilantro. Toss 1 minute. Pack over barley into 6 containers. Avocado only on the day you eat.
  • Lemon bowlsRest chicken 5 minutes. Slice. Juice of the zested lemon + parsley if you have it. Pack barley + roasted veg + chicken into 4 containers.
  • Drop oven to 400°F. Oven is now empty for the egg bake.

0:40 — egg bake

  • Toss sweet potato with 1 Tbsp oil, pepper, pinch of salt. Sheet pan B. Roast 15 minutes at 400°F.
  • Meanwhile whisk 10 large eggs, ¼ cup milk, 2 minced garlic cloves if you have them, 1 tsp smoked paprika (sweet, not hot), ½ tsp black pepper.

0:55 — peppers and onion into the pan

  • Pull the pan. Push potatoes to the side. Add diced red pepper + small onion with remaining 1 Tbsp oil. Roast 8 more minutes.

1:03 — eggs in

  • Scatter spinach over the hot veg so it wilts. Pour eggs evenly. Shake the pan once.
  • Bake 12–15 minutes until the center is just set (no wet egg). Rest 5 minutes. Cut into 6 squares. Skip feta.
  • Fridge in 6 containers. Reheat 45–60 seconds so it doesn’t turn to rubber.

1:20 — cool and store

  • Shallow containers. Fridge within 2 hours.
  • Yield: 6 egg bake + 6 fajita + 4 lemon-barley.
  • USDA leftovers: 3–4 days fridge. Freeze anything you won’t eat by Saturday.
