Recipe

Lemon chicken, barley & roasted veg

Sheet-pan chicken plus barley — the cholesterol-specific grain, not an oat jar.

4 bowls 20 min prep · 35 min oven / barley 3–4 days fridge

Two angles of the finished plate. Nothing else.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 1 cup dry pearl barley
  • 3 cups water or low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 medium head broccoli, florets
  • ½ lb carrots, sticks
  • 1 zucchini, thick half-moons
  • 1 small red onion, wedges
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • Zest + juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ½ teaspoon sweet paprika
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: parsley

Steps

  1. Rinse barley. Simmer 30–35 minutes until chewy. Drain. Cool.
  2. Heat oven to 425°F.
  3. Toss veg with 1 tablespoon oil, half the garlic, oregano, pepper.
  4. Press the thick end of the chicken flat so the whole piece is the same thickness. Toss with 1 tablespoon oil, remaining garlic, lemon zest, paprika, pepper, pinch of salt.
  5. Veg one side of the pan, chicken the other. Roast 22–28 minutes until chicken is 165°F.
  6. Rest 5 minutes. Slice. Lemon juice + parsley. Pack barley, veg, chicken.
  7. Reheat with a splash of broth, lid on, to 165°F.

Why it fits the week

White meat, skin off. Barley has the same fiber family as oats. A pile of vegetables.
